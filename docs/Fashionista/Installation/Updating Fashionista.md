## Back up your saves

Your saves are stored in MO2 under the `#Overrides - For the LOVE OF GOD DONT FUCKING TOUCH THIS` separator, inside the `{profile name} Override` mod for each profile you have played.

1. In MO2's left panel, expand the `#Overrides - For the LOVE OF GOD DONT FUCKING TOUCH THIS` separator.
2. Right-click each `{profile name} Override` mod you use and select **Open in Explorer**.
3. Copy its save folder to a location outside your Fashionista installation folder.

After backing up your saves, close MO2 and rerun the matching Steam or GOG Fashionista list in Wabbajack using your existing installation and downloads locations. Wabbajack can remove files you added to the list, so back up any personal changes you want to keep before updating.
